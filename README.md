## What this dataset answers

- **How much money do small software businesses make?** One row per product, with MRR published as a band (`< $1K`, `$1K–10K`, `$10K–100K`, `≥ $100K`) so the row stays true after the exact figure moves.
- **Which products are growing, and which are shrinking?** The 30-day trend mark sits on every row; the full report shows the channels and plays behind it.
- **What pricing model do small SaaS and AI products use?** Subscription, usage-based, marketplace, service, freemium, one-time or mixed — one column, one row per product.
- **Which categories earn the most?** The category files below list their products with the same bands.
- **Where is the full teardown?** [MoneyPeel](https://moneypeel.com/library?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=moneypeel-rankings) — one page per product, with the revenue evidence and its date.

## How to read these tables

- **MRR band, never a point.** Bands are stable enough to cite; the exact figure changes daily and lives in the report. A dash means no MRR is reported (the product may still have revenue).
- **Trend** is the 30-day amount change: three up-arrows ≥100%, two ≥30%, one up, flat, down, dot unknown.
- **Revenue is not profit.** A figure can be provider-verified and still be a GMV total. Each full report carries `revenueBasis` (what the number measures), `basisDetermination` (whether a person read it or a pricing-model rule inferred it) and `dataQuality` (whether the 30-day amount and the MRR agree).
- **Every number has a date.** The report prints `revenueAsOf` (the date the figure belongs to), `dataThrough` (what the dataset reaches) and `dataFreshness` (how far behind the schedule that is). This repository is a daily snapshot: **data as of 2026-11-27**.
- **Never derive a recurring/one-off split** from MRR divided by the 30-day amount. It is a coverage indicator, not an accounting split.

## FAQ

**What is this repository?**
The open, daily snapshot of the dataset behind MoneyPeel's business reports: one
row per product, banded revenue, growth direction, pricing model, category and a
one-line summary of the product.

**Why bands instead of exact numbers?**
A band is stable: it does not go stale the day after you cite it, and it does not
republish the point values that the reports exist to sell. If you need the exact
figure and its date, the report has both.

**Where do the numbers come from?**
Payment providers and public disclosures. Provider-verified means the number came
from a billing integration, not that the company is profitable. Revenue is not
profit, cash flow or an audited statement.

**How often is it updated?**
Daily, from the same daily pipeline the site reads. The `data as of` badge is the
date the dataset covers, not the day the script ran.

**Why do some rows show a dash for MRR?**
No MRR is reported for that product. It may still have revenue — check the report.
